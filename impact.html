Character Information

Name: Prior to the series, Rashta was sold into slavery by her own neglectful father, who did so to save himself from paying for his own crimes and abandoned her even after he paid his debts. For most of her life, she belonged to the Rimwell Estate, where she met the heir, Alan Rimwell. She became pregnant with his child, a situation which greatly angered her owner, Viscount Lotteshu. In order to "keep her in check", Lotteshu pretended to have her newborn son, Ian, killed after birth. She was told that her child had died, received a dead baby to hold, and did not get the chance to meet Ian.
